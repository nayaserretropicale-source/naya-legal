2. Data We Collect

2.1 Information You Provide

When you create an account and use Naya, we collect:

  • Identification: email, first name, last name
  • Profile picture (optional)
  • Delivery address (for your orders)
  • Phone number (for WhatsApp orders)
  • Plant photos (personal journal)
  • Community content: posts, comments, shared photos
  • Purchase history: past orders, favorite items
  • Preferences: notifications, watering reminders

2.2 Automatically Collected Information

  • Usage data: actions in the app, features used
  • Technical data: device type (iOS), OS version, language
  • Unique device identifier: for push notifications

2.3 Data We DO NOT Collect

We never collect:

  • Your precise GPS location
  • Your phone contacts
  • Your health data
  • Your banking data (payments are handled by third parties: Wave, Orange Money, MTN MoMo)
  • Data for advertising or tracking purposes
  • Continuous access to microphone or camera

3. How We Use Your Data

3.1 Essential Features

  • Create and manage your account
  • Process your orders
  • Allow you to use the plant journal
  • Enable the community (posts, likes, comments)
  • Send reminder notifications (watering, delivery)

3.2 Communication

  • Send you information about your order
  • Answer your questions via WhatsApp or email
  • Inform you of app updates

3.3 Service Improvement

  • Understand how you use the app
  • Detect and fix bugs
  • Improve existing features

We NEVER use your data for targeted advertising.

4. Sharing Your Data

4.1 Who We Share Your Data With

  • Supabase (database hosting) — based in the United States, GDPR compliant
  • Expo / Anthropic (push notifications) — based in the United States
  • Payment processors: Wave, Orange Money, MTN MoMo (when you pay for an order)
  • Delivery service: only your address to deliver your plants

4.2 Who We DO NOT Share Your Data With

  • No sale of data to third parties
  • No sharing with advertisers
  • No transfer to affiliated companies without consent

5. Community Content

When you post content in the Naya community (photos, posts, comments):

  • This content is public and visible to all app users
  • You retain ownership of your photos
  • You grant us the right to display this content in the app
  • Never post sensitive personal information in public posts

You can delete your posts at any time from the app.

6. Data Security

We protect your data through:

  • HTTPS encryption for all communications
  • Hashed passwords (never stored in plain text)
  • Row Level Security (RLS) on the Supabase database
  • Limited access: only your account sees your private data

However, no method is 100% secure. We do our best to protect your data.

7.3 Deletion

7.4 Objection

9. Push Notifications

Naya may send you notifications for:

  • Remind you to water your plants
  • Inform you about your orders
  • Notify you of likes and comments on your posts
  • News and seasonal tips

You can disable notifications at any time:

  • In the app: My Account → Notifications
  • In iOS: Settings → Notifications → Naya Serre Tropicale

10. Minors

Naya Serre Tropicale is not intended for children under 13 years old.

If you are between 13 and 18 years old, you must have permission from a parent or legal guardian to use the application.

If we discover that an account belongs to a child under 13, we will delete it immediately.

Your data may be stored outside of Côte d'Ivoire, including the United States (Supabase, Expo).

These transfers are protected by:

  • Standard Contractual Clauses approved by the European Commission

12. Cookies and Similar Technologies

Naya does NOT use cookies as it is a native mobile application.

We use a unique installation identifier to recognize you between sessions. This identifier is anonymous and contains no personal information.
